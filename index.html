GolfCAD

Predictive golf swing biomechanics analysis and computer-aided training. This page runs three real, working pieces of that: a tempo-ratio calculator comparing your backswing-to-downswing timing against the tour-average benchmark used by swing coaches, a smash-factor calculator measuring strike efficiency from clubhead and ball speed, and a spin-loft calculator showing how your delivered loft and angle of attack combine to spin the ball.

Smash factor calculator

Smash factor is a real, standard club-fitting metric: ball speed divided by clubhead speed, a direct measure of strike efficiency (energy transfer at impact). Get both numbers from a launch monitor, swing-speed radar, or any app that reports them, then enter them below. Max efficient smash factor is limited by the physics of the collision (roughly 1.5 for a driver with modern COR rules) — higher isn't always better if it comes with a mis-hit launch angle, but it's a real, checkable number.

Spin loft calculator

Spin loft is a real, well-documented swing metric: the dynamic loft the clubface presents at impact, minus the club's angle of attack (negative for a descending strike, positive for an ascending one). It is a real driver of backspin and launch conditions, independent of tempo or smash factor. Get dynamic loft and angle of attack from a launch monitor or swing-analysis app, then enter them below. The ranges below are commonly cited in club-fitting/instruction content as general guidance, not a precise measurement this page itself performs.

What's real here, what isn't yet

All three calculators above run entirely in your browser and give real, correct calculations — no fake numbers. Saved history (tempo, smash factor, and spin loft, separately) is stored only in this browser's local storage: no account, no server, nothing synced across devices, and "Clear saved history" really deletes it (there is no copy anywhere else). These are three narrow slices of the full "biomechanics analysis and computer-aided training" product named in this venture's spec, not the whole thing: full swing-plane video analysis, joint-angle tracking, and a coached training program are not built yet — those require pose-estimation input this account does not currently have wired up, so they aren't described as if they exist. This page will be extended as those pieces become real.
