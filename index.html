GolfCAD

Predictive golf swing biomechanics analysis and computer-aided training. This page runs one real, working piece of that: a tempo-ratio calculator comparing your backswing-to-downswing timing against the tour-average benchmark used by swing coaches.

Swing tempo calculator

Time your swing on video (frame count or stopwatch, in tenths of a second) and enter the two phases below. Tour average tempo is close to a 3:1 backswing:downswing ratio (source: published swing-tempo research, e.g. Tom Tomasello / trackable via most swing-analysis apps).

What's real here, what isn't yet

The tempo calculator above runs entirely in your browser and gives a real, correct ratio calculation — no fake numbers. It is one narrow slice of the full "biomechanics analysis and computer-aided training" product named in this venture's spec, not the whole thing: full swing-plane video analysis, joint-angle tracking, and a coached training program are not built yet. This page will be extended as those pieces become real, rather than described as if they already exist.

golfcad.cc — part of the golf-tech cluster alongside golflink.cc (stats & history ledger) and golfmind.cc (mental performance training).
